About VÉLISSE

VÉLISSE began as a question asked in a studio full of architecture books and no gemstones: what would jewelry look like if it were designed by someone who thought in load paths and sightlines, rather than settings and carats? The answer became our first collection, and the discipline has held ever since.

Design Principles

Structure

Every piece begins as a load-bearing idea before it becomes an ornament — weight, balance, and tension resolved first, decoration second.

Mass

We design in volume, not outline. A ring should have presence in the hand before it has presence on the finger.

Negative Space

What we leave out is deliberate. Empty space is not absence — it is where the eye is invited to rest.

Strategy

Like a well-played opening, a strong piece reveals its intent slowly — nothing is incidental, nothing is wasted.

"We don't sketch jewelry. We draft it — the way you would draft a building that happens to be worn."

— The Design Studio, VÉLISSE
